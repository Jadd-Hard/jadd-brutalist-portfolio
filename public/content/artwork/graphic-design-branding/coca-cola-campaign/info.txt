Title: COCA-COLA SOUTH AFRICA
Subtitle: CORPORATE ENGAGEMENT POSTER CAMPAIGN
Vision: My goal for this internal campaign was to transform dry corporate guidelines into visually unified touch points that motivate and align our workforce. By structuring the message around three strategic pillars, the roll-out became highly focused.
Feedback & Ethics: Driving open communication was paramount. This led to creating prominent channels for employee suggestions, news briefs, and secure ethical reporting under our Code of Business Conduct.
Growth & Wellbeing: To nurture a supportive workspace, the focus turned to employee wellness, peer appreciation, and active innovation.
Brand & Community: These designs seek to mobilise workers as proud brand ambassadors while celebrating community upliftment under the core African philosophy of Ubuntu: "I AM because WE ARE".
Creative Process: Distilling abstract corporate values into striking visual metaphors was my starting point using a vintage whistle for compliance, an energy-saving bulb for active innovation, a megaphone for praise, and mirrored silhouettes forming a chemical flask to represent a safe space for dialogue.
Visual Identity: The work is built around a bold "Retro-Modern Constructionist" aesthetic. Limiting the palette strictly to Coca-Cola red, charcoal black, white, and silver-grey established instant, logo-independent brand recognition.
Graphic Layer & Typography: To inject movement and energy, my process involved layering backgrounds with dynamic starbursts, diagonal lines, and vintage halftone textures. Elegant, hand-lettered cursive scripts contrast with the rigid geometric frames, while the centerpiece features custom 3D block typography to physically represent community interconnection.
